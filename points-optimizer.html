    Transfer Points or Pay Cash?

    Work out the cents-per-point value of a specific redemption to see whether transferring points beats just paying cash.

    Most issuers value your points at roughly 1¢ each when you redeem for cash back or a statement credit — 100 points ≈ $1. So a transfer redemption's real payoff is how far past that 1¢ baseline it gets you: right around 1¢ is a wash, comfortably above it means the transfer earned you extra value, and below it means you'd have been better off just taking the cash. That's the logic behind the four tiers below.

    Under 1.0 cpp — Bad redemption, cash wins
    1.0–1.3 cpp — OK redemption
    1.4–1.7 cpp — Good redemption
    1.8 cpp and up — Excellent redemption

Points glossary

The lingo, in plain English.

CPP (cents per point)
How much each point is worth on a booking. Divide the cash price by the points needed. Higher is better.
5/24
Chase's rule: if you've opened 5 or more personal cards from any bank in the last 24 months, you'll usually be denied for new Chase cards.
Transfer partners
Airlines and hotels you can move bank points to, often for much more value than cashing them out.
Award space
The seats or rooms an airline or hotel actually releases for points bookings. Finding four seats together is the hard part for families.
Sign-up bonus
The big batch of points a card gives you after you spend a set amount in the first few months.
